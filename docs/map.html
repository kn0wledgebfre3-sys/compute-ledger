    Map of sites

    Every site the ledger holds with coordinates from a public document: datacenters, telephone exchanges filed as datacenters, buildings that contain a datacenter, crypto mining sites moving to AI or hosting and nuclear plants. None of the smelters and refiners, chip fabs or crypto mining sites is placed yet: they are in the list below the map until a document gives their location. The colour shows what the ledger has found for a site so far, not anything about the site itself. Choose a topic to colour the map by what is found on that topic alone, or an organisation to see its sites. Click a site for its page and its figures, topic by topic.
