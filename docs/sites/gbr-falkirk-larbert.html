    Notes
    Status: planning application P/26/0237/FUL (received 29 May, validated 4 June 2026) for 'Construction of an AI Data Centre with 300MW Demand Utility Capacity', as Falkirk Council's public notice gives it (June 2026); a full application supported by an EIA. At a pre-determination hearing on 16 September 2026 the council recorded that 'the matter would be determined by the Planning Committee at a future meeting'; its minute names the applicant as Apatura DC Project 5 Ltd and says nothing of notification to or call-in by the Scottish Ministers. The applicant's planning statement (May 2026) describes 'two buildings of approximately 300-megawatt (MW) utility demand capacity' (whether per building or in total is not clear), underground diesel storage for backup generators with a 48-hour supply, closed-loop cooling and a private substation fed from two private 275 kV circuits. Placed from the grid reference 'X 285000, Y 683700' in the applicant's planning statement (a copy hosted on a campaign group's website; read twice; given to the nearest 100 m; converted from the British National Grid to WGS84).
