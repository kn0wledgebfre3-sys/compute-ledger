    Vocabulary

    The ledger’s terms

    Every class, relation and controlled list the ledger’s graph uses, with the standard term it matches where one exists (schema.org, PROV-O, QUDT). Each term’s address is this page with its name after #. Machine-readable: vocabulary.jsonld.

    Classes

    TermMeaningMatches
    CheckCheck
    A check of the ledger: what was read again, how, and what was found.
    		prov:Activity
    DocumentDocument
    A document the ledger read: a record (regulator, court, grid operator, statistics office, auditor, registry, customs) or a claim (what a company or government says).
    		schema:CreativeWork
    EntryEntry
    A ledger entry: what the records show about one subject, what is claimed, and what is missing.
    		schema:CreativeWork
    FigureFigure
    One number as a document published it, with its period, the document, and whether it is a record or a claim.
    		qudt:QuantityValue
    LinkLink
    A statement that one node relates to another, with the document that states it and the line it was read from.
    		prov:Entity
    NamedPartyNamedParty
    A party a document names that is in no register of the ledger yet.
    		schema:Thing
    OrganisationOrganisation
    A company, utility, public body or other organisation the ledger names.
    		schema:Organization
    SiteSite
    A datacenter, fab, smelter or refiner, mining site or power plant in the site register.
    		schema:Place

    Kinds of document

    TermMeaningMatches
    basis/assured_disclosureassured disclosure
    a company-reported figure covered by a third-party assurance statement; cite the statement and give its level (limited or reasonable) in notes
    basis/audit_or_courtaudit or court
    an auditor's or court's finding
    basis/company_statementcompany statement
    a party's statement in its own voice: a company's or industry body's release, report, plan or filing narrative, or a government's announcement, plan, forecast or target (as opposed to its binding decisions, statistics and gazetted rules)
    basis/derivedderived
    computed by the ledger from other rows (formula in metric_detail)
    basis/estimate_by_record_holderestimate by record holder
    an estimate made by the public body that published it (e.g., a market monitor's counterfactual)
    basis/filedfiled
    a figure a utility or operator filed with a regulator or grid operator (demand, forecast, contract)
    basis/government_documentgovernment document
    a statement of fact in a government agency's own document that is not a decision, a statistic or an estimate (for example an environmental assessment describing existing infrastructure)
    basis/inventoryinventory
    a regulator's emissions or activity inventory
    basis/legislative_documentlegislative document
    a bill, rapporteur's opinion or committee report (not enacted law)
    basis/meteredmetered
    a meter reading reported to or by a regulator, statistics office or public utility
    basis/ngo_reportngo report
    a figure from a non-governmental organisation's own investigation, monitoring or report
    basis/official_statisticofficial statistic
    a statistics office or government aggregate
    basis/peer_reviewed_studypeer reviewed study
    a figure from a peer-reviewed journal article
    basis/permittedpermitted
    a limit or rating in a permit or licence
    basis/press_reportpress report
    a figure known only through a press report of a document
    basis/registry_recordregistry record
    a record in a public registry (certificates, credits, retirements)
    basis/regulator_decisionregulator decision
    a figure in a regulator's order or decision
    basis/standard_or_methodologystandard or methodology
    a figure stated in a standard-setter's, rater's or methodology document about its own rules
    basis/utility_publishedutility published
    a figure a public utility, public power authority or grid operator published in its own documents (board papers, updates, statistics pages, presentations, letters to a regulator)

    Records are documents from regulators, courts, grid operators, statistics offices, auditors, registries or customs. Claims are what companies and governments say. A gap means the ledger has not found or read a public record yet, not that none exists or that anything is wrong. A line between two names is drawn only where a document states the link.
