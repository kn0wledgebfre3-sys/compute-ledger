    How many datacentres

    The same on every country's page.

    The ledger names 1 datacentre site in Canada, each from a permit, planning decision, filing or other public document (0 on the map).

    A commercial directory, Data Center Map, lists 306 datacentres there (read on 20 November 2026). Operators list their own facilities; the count gives no size and does not say what runs in them, so it is the directory's claim, not a record.

    Neither number says what share of the capacity serves AI: no record or listing the ledger has read states it.
