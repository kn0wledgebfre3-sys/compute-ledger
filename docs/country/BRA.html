    How many datacentres

    The same on every country's page.

    The ledger names 2 datacentre sites in Brazil, each from a permit, planning decision, filing or other public document (2 on the map).

    A commercial directory, Data Center Map, lists 243 datacentres there (read on 20 November 2026). Operators list their own facilities; the count gives no size and does not say what runs in them, so it is the directory's claim, not a record.

    Neither number says what share of the capacity serves AI: no record or listing the ledger has read states it. The directory page: Data Centers - Database (datacentres listed by country) · the entry that compares the two: How many datacentres each country has: a commercial directory's count, beside the sites public records name.
