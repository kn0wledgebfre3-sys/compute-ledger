    Records beside claims

    The ledger keeps what records show apart from what companies and governments say. This page puts the two side by side, each figure with its document. On the left, the record side: figures read from a record, such as a regulator’s decision, a permit, a filing, an assured report or an official statistic, from a report of one, or computed by the ledger from records. On the right, claims. The ledger does not say which is right, and a difference is not a finding that anything is wrong.

    The same quantity, from both sides

    Pairs in which a record and a claim state the same thing, chosen by hand and checked: 5 for the same period, 6 for different periods or with a period not stated, which the row says. Where the two print different units, both are also given in one unit. The list is in sites/views/same_quantity.csv.
