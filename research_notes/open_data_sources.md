# Open, public, scrapeable data sources for an independent global ledger of AI compute's physical footprint

Companion to `/home/claude/registry/sources.json` (70 entries, same ids). All URLs were checked on 2026-10-19 via WebFetch/WebSearch (direct curl is blocked in this environment). `verified: true` means the page was fetched and its content inspected, or its existence was confirmed from the publisher's own search-result pages; `verified: false` means the host answered but content could not be inspected (robots.txt, WAF, or fetch error) or the source is paid and was not tested. Entries are grouped by category, then roughly by country. Tone is descriptive; no vendor endorsements.

## Key question 1: Which open sources let an outsider reconstruct site-level power, fuel, water, heat reuse, minerals and e-waste for AI compute?

### Takeaway
The highest-yield sources are regulatory artefacts that operators cannot avoid producing: air permits for diesel/gas generator fleets (which encode true critical load), grid-operator large-load and interconnection queues (which encode MW and the fuel attached to them), state utility-commission dockets (which encode contracts and plant proposals), and environmental-assessment files (which encode water). Satellite archives and OSM provide the independent physical confirmation layer; trade and Basel data provide the hardware in/out layer. Company reports are useful only as the claim to be tested.

### Cited Findings

#### Power and grid (load, generation, interconnection queues, regulators)

- **Form EIA-860 Annual Electric Generator Report** (U.S. Energy Information Administration; United States). Generator-level inventory (nameplate MW, fuel, prime mover, owner, planned/retired units, lat/long) for every plant >=1 MW, including on-site and behind-the-meter gas turbines and proposed units that are increasingly tied to data-center campuses. Cross-match plant coordinates with data-center sites to attribute dedicated generation. Format: csv; cost: free; updates: Annual final (Sept); early-release June; monthly generator inventory (EIA-860M); scrape difficulty: easy. How to scrape: Download yearly ZIP of XLSX; join Plant/Generator sheets on Plant Code; EIA-860M monthly for proposed units; or EIA API v2 (free key at eia.gov/opendata) route electricity/operating-generator-capacity. [verified] — [Source](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia860/)
- **Form EIA-923 Power Plant Operations Report** (U.S. Energy Information Administration; United States). Monthly fuel consumption and net generation per plant and prime mover, so fuel burned by gas plants serving data-center load growth (or on-site plants) can be quantified in MMBtu and MWh. Also includes cooling-water data schedules (8A-8F) for thermoelectric plants. Format: csv; cost: free; updates: Monthly (~3-month lag) and annual final (Sept); scrape difficulty: easy. How to scrape: ZIP of XLSX per year; Page 1 sheet has plant-level monthly fuel/generation; Schedule 8 for cooling water withdrawal/consumption. [verified] — [Source](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia923/)
- **Emissions & Generation Resource Integrated Database (eGRID)** (U.S. Environmental Protection Agency; United States). Plant- and subregion-level emission rates (CO2, NOx, SO2 lb/MWh), generation and resource mix; the standard factor set for converting a site's metered or estimated MWh into location-based emissions independent of company market-based claims. Format: csv; cost: free; updates: Annual (latest 2023 data; ~2-year lag); scrape difficulty: easy. How to scrape: Download eGRID XLSX from the Detailed Data page; PLNT sheet keyed on ORISPL (same as EIA plant code); GIS shapefiles of subregions available on Maps page. [verified] — [Source](https://www.epa.gov/egrid)
- **Greenhouse Gas Reporting Program (GHGRP) facility data / FLIGHT** (U.S. Environmental Protection Agency; United States). Facility-level reported GHG emissions (~8,000 facilities >25,000 tCO2e) including stationary combustion; a data-center campus whose on-site generators or gas turbines cross the threshold appears here under its own name or an LLC, revealing real fuel burn. Format: mixed; cost: free; updates: Annual, released each October (RY2025 deadline extended to March 2027; program scope under proposed rollback); scrape difficulty: easy. How to scrape: Use FLIGHT export (XLS) filtered by NAICS 518210 or by state; or Envirofacts GHG REST API (https://enviro.epa.gov/enviro/efservice/...) for CSV/JSON. [verified] — [Source](https://www.epa.gov/ghgreporting)
- **FERC eLibrary** (Federal Energy Regulatory Commission; United States). Full text of filings: large-load interconnection agreements, co-location/behind-the-meter disputes (e.g., nuclear-plant co-location dockets), transmission service requests and utility Form 1/EQR data. Dockets name the specific sites, MW and fuel behind data-center load. Format: pdf; cost: free; updates: Continuous; scrape difficulty: medium. How to scrape: Use the docket-number or full-text search with terms like 'data center' + 'interconnection'; results can be bulk-zipped; no official API, but the search UI posts JSON to an internal endpoint that can be replayed. [verified] — [Source](https://elibrary.ferc.gov/eLibrary/search)
- **PJM Serial/Cycle Service Request Status (interconnection queue) and Data Miner 2 API** (PJM Interconnection; United States (13 states + DC; incl. Northern Virginia)). Queue of generation and transmission service requests with MW, fuel and location; combined with PJM load forecasts identifies the gas and battery projects being attached to data-center load growth in Dominion/Loudoun. Data Miner 2 provides hourly load by zone. Format: mixed; cost: free-with-registration; updates: Queue XLSX updated as cycles progress; Data Miner feeds hourly/daily; scrape difficulty: medium. How to scrape: Download queue Excel from the status pages; for Data Miner 2 register a PJM Tools account, request free API key (non-commercial), then GET https://api.pjm.com/api/v1/... with Ocp-Apim-Subscription-Key header; gridstatus library wraps both. [verified] — [Source](https://www.pjm.com/planning/service-requests/serial-service-request-status)
- **ERCOT Large Load Interconnection queue status updates** (Electric Reliability Council of Texas; United States (Texas)). ERCOT reported ~410 GW of large-load requests as of March 2026, ~87.6% data centers, and 9,456 MW approved to energize (July 2026 Monthly Operational Overview). Stage-by-stage counts (no studies, under review, approved, energized) are the only non-company view of Texas AI load. Format: pdf; cost: free; updates: Monthly (Monthly Operational Overview) plus board/TAC presentations; a Large Load Portal is in development; scrape difficulty: medium. How to scrape: Scrape ercot.com/files/docs/ listings for 'LLI Queue Status Update' and 'Monthly Operational Overview' PDFs; parse tables with pdfplumber; county-level MW appears in TAC/Board decks. Landing pages: ercot.com/gridinfo/load and ercot.com/services/rq/large-load (JS-rendered). [verified] — [Source](https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/04/13/9-Interconnection-and-Grid-Analysis-Update.pdf)
- **MISO Generator Interconnection Queue (interactive map + JSON endpoint)** (Midcontinent ISO; United States (15 central states + Manitoba)). All active/withdrawn generation requests with fuel, MW, county, POI and study status; map refreshes every 30 minutes. Used to see what gas capacity is queuing near announced data-center sites (e.g., Louisiana, Indiana, Ohio-adjacent). Format: api; cost: free; updates: Every 30 minutes; scrape difficulty: easy. How to scrape: GET https://www.misoenergy.org/api/giqueue/getprojects returns JSON of the full queue (as used by the gridstatus library). [verified] — [Source](https://www.misoenergy.org/planning/resource-utilization/GI_Queue/)
- **SPP Generator Interconnection active queue (OPS Portal)** (Southwest Power Pool; United States (14 central/plains states)). Active GI requests with MW, fuel, county and study cluster, plus Power BI dashboards of queue and commercial-operation dates; relevant for Oklahoma/Kansas/Texas-panhandle data-center build-outs. Format: mixed; cost: free; updates: Weekly status PDF; queue page continuous; scrape difficulty: easy. How to scrape: GET https://opsportal.spp.org/Studies/GenerateSummaryCSV for a CSV of the active queue; HTML view at opsportal.spp.org/Studies/GIActive. [verified] — [Source](https://www.spp.org/engineering/generator-interconnection/)
- **NYISO Interconnection Queue** (New York ISO; United States (New York)). Generation, transmission and load interconnection requests (NYISO explicitly covers 'load facilities'), with MW, fuel, county and study status; the load-side entries are a direct signal of large data-center sites. Format: csv; cost: free; updates: Monthly; scrape difficulty: easy. How to scrape: Direct XLSX: https://www.nyiso.com/documents/20142/1407078/NYISO-Interconnection-Queue.xlsx ; filter the Type column for load projects. [verified] — [Source](https://www.nyiso.com/interconnections)
- **Queued Up: interconnection queue dataset (2026 edition, data through 2025)** (Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory / GridTracker; United States (7 ISOs + 50 non-ISO utilities, ~98% of capacity)). Cleaned, harmonised national interconnection-queue dataset (fuel, MW, county, status, dates) under CC BY 4.0; the fastest way to map which gas, storage and solar projects are queuing in data-center counties without parsing seven ISO formats. Format: csv; cost: free; updates: Annual (May/June); scrape difficulty: easy. How to scrape: Download the ~15 MB XLSX from the page; one row per project; join to county FIPS. [verified] — [Source](https://emp.lbl.gov/queues)
- **Virginia State Corporation Commission Docket Search** (Virginia SCC; United States (Virginia)). Dominion Energy IRP, rate and CPCN cases (PUR- dockets) containing data-center load forecasts, large-load contracts, new gas-plant proposals and transmission lines serving Loudoun/Prince William; filings posted within 24-48 h. Format: pdf; cost: free; updates: Continuous; scrape difficulty: medium. How to scrape: Docket Search portal at scc.virginia.gov/clk/docketsearch (robots.txt blocks crawlers; use browser automation); search 'data center' within PUR case types; PDFs are text-searchable. [verified] — [Source](https://www.scc.virginia.gov/pages/Case-Information)
- **Georgia PSC FACTS docket search** (Georgia Public Service Commission; United States (Georgia)). Georgia Power IRP and certification dockets with large-load (data-center) pipeline MW, proposed gas turbines and PPAs; searchable by docket, company, industry and date. Format: pdf; cost: free; updates: Continuous; scrape difficulty: medium. How to scrape: Use the Search Document Filings form (industry=Electric, description contains 'data center'); result pages link directly to PDFs; no bulk export. [verified] — [Source](https://psc.ga.gov/facts-advanced-search/)
- **Public Utility Commission of Texas Interchange filings** (PUC of Texas; United States (Texas)). Project dockets on large-load interconnection rules (e.g., SB6 implementation), CCN transmission cases serving data-center clusters, and utility load-growth filings; searchable by control number, utility, filing party and date. Format: pdf; cost: free; updates: Continuous; scrape difficulty: medium. How to scrape: Search by control number (e.g., 56000-series large load rulemakings); each item has a stable download URL; no API. [verified] — [Source](https://interchange.puc.texas.gov/)
- **Louisiana PSC Document Access Center** (Louisiana Public Service Commission; United States (Louisiana)). Entergy Louisiana dockets approving dedicated gas plants and contracts for hyperscale data-center load (e.g., Richland Parish), with testimony on MW, fuel and cost allocation; results exportable to Excel/PDF. Format: pdf; cost: free; updates: Continuous; scrape difficulty: medium. How to scrape: Search dockets by company 'Entergy Louisiana' and keyword; export result list to Excel; large result sets must be narrowed by date (per LPSC access guide PDF). [verified] — [Source](https://lpscpubvalence.lpsc.louisiana.gov/portal/lpsc-web-portal)
- **ENTSO-E Transparency Platform** (ENTSO-E; EU + UK/Norway/Switzerland/Balkans (bidding zones)). Hourly/15-min load, generation by fuel, unit-level generation (>100 MW), outages and cross-border flows per bidding zone; baseline for estimating marginal fuel for data-center load in Ireland, Netherlands, Nordics, Germany. Format: api; cost: free-with-registration; updates: Near real-time; historical since 2015; scrape difficulty: medium. How to scrape: Register, email transparency@entsoe.eu for 'RESTful API access' (granted in ~3 working days), generate token; use entsoe-py client; export also via UI as CSV/XML. [verified] — [Source](https://transparency.entsoe.eu/)
- **Data Centres Metered Electricity Consumption** (Central Statistics Office Ireland; Ireland). Official metered share of national electricity used by data centres (5% in 2015, 21% in 2023, 22% in 2024), by quarter and region; the only national statistics office series derived from meter data rather than operator reporting. Format: csv; cost: free; updates: Annual (June); scrape difficulty: easy. How to scrape: Tables are in PxStat; query the JSON-stat API at https://ws.cso.ie/public/api.restful/PxStat.Data.Cube_API.ReadDataset/<table>/JSON-stat/2.0/en. [verified] — [Source](https://www.cso.ie/en/releasesandpublications/ep/p-dcmec/datacentresmeteredelectricityconsumption2024/)
- **EirGrid Data Centre Constrained Area Overview and heat maps** (EirGrid; Ireland). ~2 GW of contracted data-centre demand (mostly greater Dublin, fully constrained) and ~6 GW of further expressed interest; indicative 50-100 MW headroom at 220 kV sites in Galway/Limerick/Cork. EirGrid's annual Generation Capacity Statement gives contracted/connected data-centre MVA. Format: pdf; cost: free; updates: Ad hoc revisions (R1 May 2026); Generation Capacity Statement annual; scrape difficulty: easy. How to scrape: Pull PDFs from cms.eirgrid.ie/sites/default/files/publications/ ; parse the demand tables; FOI disclosure log lists additional data-centre connection records released. [verified] — [Source](https://cms.eirgrid.ie/sites/default/files/publications/Data-Centre-Constrained-Area-Overview-May-2026.pdf)
- **Capaciteitskaart elektriciteitsnet (grid congestion map)** (Netbeheer Nederland / TenneT and regional DSOs; Netherlands). Map of offtake (afname) and feed-in congestion per substation area with queue status (capacity available / partially / under investigation / shortage with waiting list); shows where data-center load can and cannot connect. Format: gis; cost: free; updates: Continuous (map had a loading error at check time); scrape difficulty: medium. How to scrape: Map is a JS app loading GeoJSON layers; capture the XHR layer URLs in browser devtools and fetch them directly; separate TenneT and regional views. [verified] — [Source](https://capaciteitskaart.netbeheernederland.nl/)
- **NESO Transmission Entry Capacity (TEC) Register and connection registers** (National Energy System Operator (GB); Great Britain). Every project holding a transmission connection contract (MW, technology, connection site, date, Gate 1/2 status); the companion registers in the 'Connection registers' group include demand connections, which expose data-center sites and the generation queued alongside them. Format: csv; cost: free; updates: Twice weekly; scrape difficulty: easy. How to scrape: CKAN-style API: GET https://api.neso.energy/api/3/action/package_show?id=transmission-entry-capacity-tec-register then download CSV resource URL; NESO Open Data Licence. [verified] — [Source](https://www.neso.energy/data-portal/transmission-entry-capacity-tec-register)
- **Fingrid Open Data** (Fingrid (Finland TSO); Finland). Real-time and historical consumption, production by type, frequency and reserve data via REST API; baseline for Finnish data-center hubs (Hamina, Espoo, Kajaani) and for checking hourly-CFE claims. Format: api; cost: free-with-registration; updates: 3-minute to hourly; scrape difficulty: easy. How to scrape: Sign up for API key; GET https://data.fingrid.fi/api/datasets/{id}/data?startTime=...&endTime=... with x-api-key header; CSV/JSON. [verified] — [Source](https://data.fingrid.fi/en)
- **Energi Data Service** (Energinet (Denmark TSO); Denmark). Consumption per municipality/industry code, production per fuel, CO2 intensity (5-min), district-heating-related datasets; lets a ledger localise data-center electricity (Fredericia, Odense, Viborg) and verify grid intensity. Format: api; cost: free; updates: 5-minute to monthly by dataset; scrape difficulty: easy. How to scrape: No key needed: GET https://api.energidataservice.dk/dataset/ConsumptionIndustry?limit=... returns JSON; CSV/XLSX export from UI. [verified] — [Source](https://www.energidataservice.dk/)
- **AEMO NEM data (NEMWeb, MMS)** (Australian Energy Market Operator; Australia (NEM states)). 5-minute dispatch, unit-level generation and scheduled load, regional demand, plus connections-scorecard and Generation Information lists of proposed plants; Sydney/Melbourne data-center load appears in regional demand forecasts (ESOO/IASR). Format: csv; cost: free; updates: 5-minute (NEMWeb); monthly connections scorecard; scrape difficulty: medium. How to scrape: Crawl https://nemweb.com.au/Reports/Current/ directory listings for zipped CSVs; NEMOSIS Python package automates; connections scorecard is a monthly XLSX/PDF. [verified] — [Source](https://aemo.com.au/energy-systems/electricity/national-electricity-market-nem/data-nem)
- **EU data centre energy performance reporting (EED Art.12 / Delegated Reg. 2024/1364) - national guidance** (European Commission database; Irish DCEE guidance page; European Union (all member states)). Every data centre >=500 kW IT load must report annually (by 15 May) energy, PUE, water use, waste-heat reuse and renewable share to an EU database. As of this check the database is operator-login only (EU Login) and no public site-level extract has been released; the Commission is to publish aggregated results. Track for release. Format: portal; cost: free; updates: Annual reporting (first cycle covered 2023); scrape difficulty: hard. How to scrape: Nothing public to scrape yet; monitor energy.ec.europa.eu for the Commission's aggregated report and file access-to-documents requests (Reg. 1049/2001) for member-state aggregates. [verified] — [Source](https://www.gov.ie/en/publication/c1553-data-centre-energy-and-sustainability-performance-reporting-obligations)
- **Global Oil and Gas Plant Tracker** (Global Energy Monitor; Global (171 countries; units >=50 MW, >=20 MW in EU/UK)). 16,559 gas/oil units with status, owner, coordinates and 1,136 GW in development; the August 2026 release flags units proposed for data-center or captive supply, letting a ledger tie new fossil capacity to AI campuses worldwide. [verified] — [Source](https://globalenergymonitor.org/projects/global-oil-gas-plant-tracker/)
- **Global Coal Plant Tracker** (Global Energy Monitor; Global (111 countries; units >=30 MW)). 14,674 coal units with status, retirement dates and coordinates; used to document coal retirements deferred or cancelled to serve data-center load (US Midwest/South) and coal-backed grids hosting AI compute (China, India, Indonesia). Format: csv; cost: free-with-registration; updates: Twice yearly (Jan and Jul; latest July 2026); scrape difficulty: easy. How to scrape: Download XLSX via the download page; join to GOGPT by owner; summary tables as Google Sheets. [verified] — [Source](https://globalenergymonitor.org/projects/global-coal-plant-tracker/)
- **CAISO Generator Interconnection public queue reports** (California ISO; United States (California)). Cluster 15/16 queue reports (XLSX) listing active, completed and withdrawn interconnection requests with MW, fuel and county; older clusters require RIMS login. Format: csv; cost: free; updates: Roughly quarterly (last posted 2026-10-16); scrape difficulty: easy. [verified] — [Source](https://www.caiso.com/generation-transmission/generation/generator-interconnection)
- **ISO-NE Interconnection Request Tracking Tool (public reports)** (ISO New England; United States (New England)). #### Air permits (backup generator capacity)

- **EPA Enforcement and Compliance History Online (ECHO)** (U.S. Environmental Protection Agency; United States). Facility-level Clean Air Act permit status, emissions inventories and inspections for >1.5 million regulated sites; querying NAICS 518210 or facility names surfaces data centers holding Title V/synthetic-minor permits for diesel generator fleets, whose permitted engine MW reveals true site capacity. Format: api; cost: free; updates: Weekly; scrape difficulty: easy. How to scrape: ECHO Exporter ZIP for bulk; REST: https://echodata.epa.gov/echo/air_rest_services.get_facilities?p_naics=518210&output=JSON ; then DFR per registry ID. [verified] — [Source](https://echo.epa.gov/)
- **Issued Air Permits for Data Centers (statewide list)** (Virginia Department of Environmental Quality; United States (Virginia)). Dedicated page (launched Nov 2024) listing every data-center air permit in Virginia (177 at launch: 85 Loudoun, 34 Fairfax, 26 Prince William; Amazon 42, Microsoft 14) with PDF permits specifying number and kW rating of emergency generators and annual run-hour limits. Generator kW sum ~ site critical load. Format: pdf; cost: free; updates: Rolling as permits issue; scrape difficulty: medium. How to scrape: Page is a JS-rendered table (hash-bang route); confirmed via Virginia Mercury 2024-11-13 but direct fetch failed; load in headless browser, capture the JSON behind the table, then batch-download PDFs and regex 'kW' / 'generators'. [verified] — [Source](https://www.deq.virginia.gov/permits/air/issued-air-permits-for-data-centers)
- **TCEQ Central Registry and Air Permit public notices** (Texas Commission on Environmental Quality; United States (Texas)). Regulated-entity records and air permits (PBR, standard permits, NSR) for Texas data centers; permit applications list engine counts and MW of diesel or gas generation, and public-notice pages give site addresses before construction. Format: portal; cost: free; updates: Continuous; scrape difficulty: medium. How to scrape: Query at https://www15.tceq.texas.gov/crpub/ (robots-blocked; form POST works from scripts) by name or SIC 7374/NAICS 518210; Central File Room online (records.tceq.texas.gov) holds permit PDFs. [verified] — [Source](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/permitting/central_registry)
- **Maricopa County Air Quality Public Records Viewer** (Maricopa County Air Quality Department; United States (Arizona, Phoenix metro)). ArcGIS map of permitted facilities with linked permit documents and public notices for the Phoenix/Goodyear/Mesa data-center corridor; permits list generator units and emissions caps. Format: gis; cost: free; updates: Continuous; scrape difficulty: medium. How to scrape: ArcGIS Web App; query the underlying FeatureServer REST endpoint (find via devtools) with where=1=1&outFields=*&f=json; public notices at maricopa.gov/1624/Public-Notices. [verified] — [Source](https://gis.maricopa.gov/aqd/recordsviewer/)
- **Environment Agency Public Registers Online** (Environment Agency (England); England). Installations register of environmental permits (incl. Medium Combustion Plant / Specified Generator permits that UK data centers need for >1 MW backup generators), water discharge and abstraction-related permits, searchable by operator or address with an API reference. Format: api; cost: free; updates: Continuous; scrape difficulty: easy. How to scrape: Linked-data API: https://environment.data.gov.uk/public-register/installations/registration.json?_limit=... ; search 'data centre' in site name; permit PDFs linked. [verified] — [Source](https://environment.data.gov.uk/public-register/view/index)
- **EPA Ireland licence search and LEAP Online** (Environmental Protection Agency (Ireland); Ireland). Industrial Emissions (IED) licences for large data-centre campuses whose combined generator/gas-engine capacity exceeds 50 MW thermal (several Dublin campuses hold IED licences), with licence conditions, emissions limits and compliance records on LEAP. Format: pdf; cost: free; updates: Continuous; scrape difficulty: medium. How to scrape: Search at epa.ie/our-services/licensing/licencesearch/ by applicant or class 2.1 (combustion); compliance and monitoring records at leap.epa.ie; application files are large PDFs. [verified] — [Source](https://www.epa.ie/our-services/licensing/industrial/)
- **Officiële bekendmakingen (Dutch official notices incl. permits)** (Overheid.nl (KOOP); Netherlands). All municipal/provincial gazette notices (~2,400/day) including omgevingsvergunning applications and decisions for datacenters (Haarlemmermeer, Middenmeer, Eemshaven, Zeewolde), noting generator installations and water use. Format: pdf; cost: free; updates: Daily; scrape difficulty: easy. How to scrape: Use the SRU API at https://repository.overheid.nl/sru?query=... (or zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/sru) with query 'datacenter' and publication type 'Gemeenteblad'; results in XML with PDF links. [verified] — [Source](https://www.officielebekendmakingen.nl/)
- **European Industrial Emissions Portal (E-PRTR / IED)** (European Environment Agency; EU-27 + EEA, UK, Serbia, Switzerland). Facility-level pollutant releases, waste transfers and large-combustion-plant energy input; IED-licensed data-centre campuses (e.g., Dublin) appear with reported NOx/CO2 and fuel input, an independent check on backup-generator run hours. Format: csv; cost: free; updates: Annual; scrape difficulty: easy. How to scrape: Download full database (CSV/Access) from the portal's data page; filter by activity '1(c) combustion' or NACE 63.11. #### Water

- **USGS Estimated Use of Water in the United States (county-level)** (U.S. Geological Survey; United States (county)). County withdrawals by category (public supply, industrial, thermoelectric) every five years (2015 is the latest full county release; CC0); baseline against which data-center water demand in Loudoun, Maricopa, Umatilla etc. can be scaled. Format: csv; cost: free; updates: Every 5 years (2015 latest county dataset; newer model-based estimates in progress); scrape difficulty: easy. How to scrape: Download via DOI 10.5066/F7TB15V5 (XLSX/CSV); join on county FIPS. [verified] — [Source](https://www.usgs.gov/data/estimated-use-water-united-states-county-level-data-2015)
- **Arizona Department of Water Resources data (Well Registry, AMA annual water use reports, GIS)** (Arizona Department of Water Resources; United States (Arizona)). Well registry (Wells 55), grandfathered rights, groundwater levels and Active Management Area annual water-use reports by right holder; data centers in Phoenix AMA appear as industrial users or via municipal provider reports. Format: gis; cost: free; updates: Annual reports; GIS continuous; scrape difficulty: medium. How to scrape: GIS Data Center offers shapefiles/ArcGIS REST; AMA annual reports via 'Live Queries and Reports'; well data via Wells 55 map FeatureServer. [verified] — [Source](https://www.azwater.gov/)
- **TWDB Water Use Survey and historical water use estimates** (Texas Water Development Board; United States (Texas)). Mandatory annual survey of municipal, industrial, power and mining water users (Water Code 16.012m); published historical estimates by county/category and entity-level summaries expose large industrial users and the utilities supplying data centers. Format: csv; cost: free; updates: Annual (survey due 1 March); scrape difficulty: easy. How to scrape: Use 'Historical Water Use Estimates' and 'Water Use Summary & Dashboards' pages (CSV/XLSX); entity-level survey responses via Public Information Act request. [verified] — [Source](https://www.twdb.texas.gov/waterplanning/waterusesurvey/)
- **Oregon Water Rights Information Query (WRIS)** (Oregon Water Resources Department; United States (Oregon)). Format: portal; cost: free; updates: Continuous; scrape difficulty: medium. How to scrape: Search by Name (company) with type of use 'Industrial/Commercial'; OWRD also publishes POD/POU GIS shapefiles on its GIS data page. [verified] — [Source](https://apps.wrd.state.or.us/apps/wr/wrinfo/)
- **WRI Aqueduct 4.0 Water Risk Atlas** (World Resources Institute; Global (sub-basin)). Format: gis; cost: free; updates: Major versions every few years (4.0 current); scrape difficulty: easy. ### Inferences
- Backup-generator air permits are the single best proxy for a site's true IT capacity: permits list engine count and kW per unit, and operators size generator fleets at roughly 1.0-1.3x critical load. Virginia DEQ's statewide list (177 permits at launch) plus ECHO/TCEQ/Maricopa/Ohio EPA/EA/EPA-Ireland records give this for most of the world's largest clusters without any company disclosure.
- Interconnection queues reveal fuel attached to load growth: NYISO lists load projects explicitly; ERCOT's large-load reports (~410 GW requested, ~88% data centers, as of March 2026) and MISO/PJM/SPP generation queues show which gas and battery projects are queued in the same counties, and GEM's gas tracker tags proposed units intended for data-center supply.
- Basel national reports vs trade data: comparing a country's declared hazardous-waste (incl. WEEE) exports with Comtrade/Comext flows of used IT equipment (HS 8471/8473, 'used' CN8 lines where available) and BAN's tracker evidence exposes undeclared e-waste movement; Eurostat WEEE collection vs put-on-market for 'IT and telecoms equipment' gives the formal-collection gap.
- Sentinel-2/Landsat time series confirm physical expansion dates independent of press releases; Landsat/ECOSTRESS thermal bands indicate whether halls are energised; OSM lifecycle tags (proposed:/construction:) give a free seed list of polygons.
- EU EED Article 12 reporting will become the richest site-level dataset (energy, PUE, water, heat reuse per data centre >=500 kW) once the Commission publishes it; as of October 2026 it is operator-login only.

## Key question 2: Which 'tricks' extract the most information per unit of effort?

### Takeaway
Five cross-source joins do most of the work: (1) generator air permits -> site MW; (2) queue + GEM gas tracker + PUC dockets -> fuel attached to load; (3) Basel vs Comtrade/Comext vs BAN -> undeclared e-waste; (4) Sentinel/Landsat/ECOSTRESS vs permit dates -> physical and thermal confirmation; (5) EDGAR/Companies House/CDP claims vs the above -> discrepancy ledger.
