# People, Land, Money: social, economic, land and circularity dimensions of AI compute (public-record ledger)

Scope: open government, regulator, court, auditor, statistical-office, UN/ILO/OECD and legally bound corporate filing sources only. As of 2026-10-19. Tone neutral; "gap" lines record discrepancies factually. Sites already covered elsewhere in the ledger (Google Cerrillos, Google Canelones, xAI Memphis, Dominion IRP, Singapore moratorium, Johor water rejections, Microsoft Espoo heat, Agbogbloshie, DRC cobalt USGS) are avoided or referenced only for a different dimension.

## Category 1 — Community (bills, noise, land disputes)

### Takeaway
Two regulators in the US (Virginia JLARC; PJM's Independent Market Monitor) have put numbers on how datacenter load affects other customers' bills; Ireland's CRU has responded with a connection policy that shifts generation obligations to datacenters rather than quantifying consumer cost; Australia's AEMO quantifies demand but not cost. Noise is documented at the ordinance level (Chandler AZ, Loudoun VA) with no public measured-dB datasets. Land disputes are documented in the Netherlands (Zeewolde, state land) and Chile (Huechuraba, environmental tribunal).

### Cited Findings

**US / Virginia — JLARC Report 598 (Dec 9, 2024)**
- Typical Dominion residential customer could see generation + transmission costs rise an estimated $14–$37 per month by 2040 (constant dollars), attributable to datacenter-driven demand under the unconstrained scenario — [JLARC Rpt 598, Summary p. vi](https://jlarc.virginia.gov/pdfs/reports/Rpt598.pdf)
- Virginia datacenters use ~5,050 MW (≈2 million households); unconstrained demand could double within 10 years — [JLARC Rpt 598, pp. 5–6, 28](https://jlarc.virginia.gov/pdfs/reports/Rpt598.pdf)
- One-third of datacenters are near residential areas; low-frequency noise "rarely violates local noise ordinances", whose maximum civil penalty is $500; a large majority generate no noise complaints — [JLARC Rpt 598, Summary p. viii](https://jlarc.virginia.gov/pdfs/reports/Rpt598.pdf)
- ~150 sites, ~340 buildings, >63 million sq ft on ~7,200 acres (~2,914 ha) — [JLARC Rpt 598, p. 5](https://jlarc.virginia.gov/pdfs/reports/Rpt598.pdf)

**US / PJM — Independent Market Monitor (Monitoring Analytics)**
- 2026/2027 Base Residual Auction revenues were $16,124,370,889; including 11,993 MW of existing and forecast datacenter load added $7,271,197,971, an 82.1% increase over a no-datacenter-load scenario ($8,853,172,918) — [IMM Analysis of the 2026/2027 RPM BRA Part A, Oct 1, 2025](https://www.monitoringanalytics.com/reports/Reports/2025/IMM_Analysis_of_the_20262027_RPM_Base_Residual_Auction_Part_A_20251001.pdf)
- 2025/2026 BRA: datacenter load increased revenues by $9,332,103,858 (cited from IMM Part G Revised); combined two-auction increase $16,603,301,829 — [same IMM report](https://www.monitoringanalytics.com/reports/Reports/2025/IMM_Analysis_of_the_20262027_RPM_Base_Residual_Auction_Part_A_20251001.pdf)
- Method: scenario counterfactuals removing datacenter load from the summer peak forecast; described as estimates, not forecasts — [same](https://www.monitoringanalytics.com/reports/Reports/2025/IMM_Analysis_of_the_20262027_RPM_Base_Residual_Auction_Part_A_20251001.pdf)
- Total PJM capacity charges rose from $2,834 million (2024) to $10,616 million (2025), +262.3%; IMM recommends "Large data centers should be required to bring their own new generation" — [2025 State of the Market Report for PJM, Apr 24, 2026](https://www.pjm.com/-/media/DotCom/committees-groups/committees/mc/2026/20260424-special/2025-annual-state-of-the-market-report-for-pjm.pdf)
- Datacenter load in forecasts (embedded / embedded+above): 4,654 & 7,927 MW (2025/26); 7,892 & 11,993 MW (2026/27); 13,018 & 17,071 MW (2027/28) — [IMM presentation to PJM MC, Jan 22, 2026, slide 9](https://www.pjm.com/-/media/DotCom/committees-groups/committees/mc/2026/20260122/20260122-item-06---market-monitoring-report---presentation.pdf)

**Ireland — CRU Large Energy Users connection policy**
- Proposed decision (CRU/202504, 18 Feb 2025): datacenters ≈21% of national demand in 2023 (≈7.1 TWh of 30.5 TWh); ≈30% by 2030–32; ≈50% of electricity produced in Dublin/Meath consumed by datacenters; the paper does not quantify network-cost impacts on consumers — [CRU/202504](https://consult.cru.ie/en/system/files/materials/200/CRU202504%20LEU%20connection%20policy%20proposed%20decision_0.pdf)
- Final decision CRU/2025236 (12 Dec 2025): datacenters with MIC ≥10 MVA must provide dispatchable generation/storage ≥ MIC (de-rated), separately metered; must meet ≥80% of annual demand with additional Irish renewable generation on a 6-year glide path; new datacenters may bid only for one-year capacity contracts; 97% of Irish datacenters are in Dublin — [CRU/2025236 decision (copy hosted by Matheson)](https://media.matheson.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Large-Energy-Users-Connection-Policy-Decision-3.pdf)
- Note: the official cru.ie PDF link for CRU/2025236 was not retrieved; the Matheson-hosted copy is a verbatim reproduction. Treat as medium confidence until the cru.ie link is confirmed.

**Australia — AEMO 2026 ESOO datacenter forecasting overview**
- Datacenter consumption ≈3% of NEM operational consumption in FY26; forecast ~15 TWh (7.8%) by FY30 and ~34 TWh (13%) by FY36 (Step Change); 225 projects / 67 GW in development; 36% of projects in the 2025 ESOO were cancelled; grid connection utilisation averaged ~27% in FY26; no consumer cost figure; reforms to cost allocation for large new loads "under consideration" — [AEMO 2026 ESOO datacentre forecasting overview](https://www.aemo.com.au/-/media/files/electricity/nem/planning_and_forecasting/nem_esoo/2026/2026-esoo-data-centre-forecasting-overview.pdf)

**Netherlands — ACM**
- ACM's Oct 21, 2024 statement supports usage-based network tariffs for small consumers (from ~2028); it does not discuss datacenters or quantify € per household — [ACM, 21 Oct 2024](https://www.acm.nl/nl/publicaties/acm-steunt-het-uitgangspunt-om-netkosten-eerlijker-te-verdelen-over-kleinverbruikers). No ACM finding attributing consumer cost to datacenters was found.

**Noise — Chandler, Arizona (Ordinance No. 5033)**
- Ordinance 5033 (P&Z hearing Oct 19, 2022; published Dec 2022) prohibits standalone datacenters unless approved via a Planned Area Development; requires third-party baseline sound study before neighbourhood meetings, design so that facility sound "does not exceed the baseline ambient levels" (verified by a third-party engineer), post-construction noise study then annual studies for five years, an on-site noise liaison 8am–10pm on City request, and generator test notice 24h ahead with routine testing limited to 9–5 weekdays. No numeric dB limit or setback is set. — [City of Chandler Ordinance No. 5033](https://www.chandleraz.gov/sites/default/files/departments/development-services/PLH22-0053-Ordinance-No-5033-Data-Center.pdf); [City notice that ordinance is in effect](https://www.chandleraz.gov/index%2Ephp/news-center/chandlers-data-center-ordinance-now-effect)

**Noise — Loudoun County, Virginia**
- County page: residential receiving-property limit 55 dBA; "The county does not currently have policy or regulatory language specific to tonal noise or low frequency noise"; pre-construction noise study at site plan and post-construction study 1–12 months after first CO required; Va. Code §15.2-2209.5 requires a site assessment and sound profile for legislative applications ≥100 MW; comprehensive plan/zoning amendments on tonal and low-frequency noise anticipated spring/summer 2027; no complaint counts or measured levels published — [Loudoun County, Data Centers: Noise & Air Quality Concerns](https://www.loudoun.gov/6405/Noise-Air-Quality-Concerns)

**Land dispute — Zeewolde, Netherlands (Meta)**
- 166 ha site; ~80 ha state-owned (Rijksvastgoedbedrijf); Senate (Eerste Kamer) motion adopted Dec 2021 that the State may not sell land for large-scale datacenters; interpellation 22 Feb 2022 recorded that "only 15% of residual heat is reused" per the plan and that the municipal permit would not be revoked — [Eerste Kamer, Interpellatie grootschalige datacentra, 22 Feb 2022](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/nieuws/20220222/interpellatie_grootschalige)
- Minister De Jonge letter to Tweede Kamer, 10 June 2022 (Kamerstuk 26 643 nr. 862): TNO assessed the municipality's plan against the August 2021 sale conditions (maximum solar, residual-heat reuse, etc.) — [Kamerstuk 26643-862](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-26643-862.pdf); NOS reported the same day that the State would not sell because conditions were not met and TNO found no guarantee of residual-heat reuse — [NOS, 10 June 2022](https://nos.nl/l/2432182) (older source, 2022)

**Land/community dispute — Huechuraba, Chile (AWS)**
- RCA for "Centro de Almacenamiento de Datos Huechuraba" (Servicios Amazon Data Services Chile) approved 12 July 2024; 10.9 ha site, 21,350 m² built, US$205 million, 30-year design life. Residents sought annulment (late 2024); SEA rejected the administrative claims April 2025; Segundo Tribunal Ambiental upheld the RCA (ruling reported 6 April 2026), finding no "relación de interdependencia funcional" with the high-voltage line and that any future HV infrastructure must undergo its own evaluation including cumulative impacts — [Emol, 6 Apr 2026 (reporting the Tribunal ruling)](https://www.emol.com/noticias/Economia/2026/04/06/1196414/amazon-tribunal-ambiental.html). The Tribunal's own judgment text was not retrieved — medium confidence.

### Inferences
- The IMM's $16.6 bn two-auction figure is a counterfactual revenue estimate, not an audited consumer bill impact; PJM has not adopted it as a finding.
- Noise regulation in both Chandler and Loudoun relies on relative/ambient or dBA standards; neither jurisdiction publishes measured datasets, so "noise complaints exist" is documentable but "noise exceeds limits" generally is not.

### Gaps
- No Asian regulator finding attributing retail rate increases to datacenters was found in official sources (Johor water cases are excluded by scope).
- Pennsylvania/Maryland/New Jersey consumer-advocate filings were not individually retrieved.
- No official measured dB datasets for Loudoun or Dublin complaints were found.

## Category 2 — Public money

### Takeaway
Audited or legislatively costed subsidy figures exist for Virginia (JLARC: $928.6m FY23 sales-tax saving), Georgia (DOAA: $474.2m FY25, with a January 2026 correction cutting attributed jobs by two-thirds), Brazil (Senate budget note: R$5.2 bn 2026 renúncia for Redata) and Uttar Pradesh (notified policy with capped subsidies). Texas JETI has an SAO audit but it is a process audit with no datacenter figures.

### Cited Findings

**US / Virginia — sales and use tax exemption**
- Datacenters and tenants saved ≈$928.6 million in sales taxes in FY23 (state share ≈$683m); ~30 companies claim it, median $5.4m; ~90% of industry uses it; expires 2035 — [JLARC Rpt 598, pp. 20–21](https://jlarc.virginia.gov/pdfs/reports/Rpt598.pdf)
- In exchange: industry supported ~74,000 jobs/yr (FY21–23 average), $9.1 bn GDP; direct ~39,400; operations ~15,000 jobs; local tax revenue 31% of Loudoun's and 7% ($110m) of Prince William's total — [JLARC Rpt 598, Table 2-1, p. 16](https://jlarc.virginia.gov/pdfs/reports/Rpt598.pdf); [PWC summary](https://pwcva.gov/assets/2024-12/JLARC%20Report%20Summary_12-16-2024.pdf)

**US / Georgia — Department of Audits and Accounts**
- Forgone state revenue $474.2m in FY2025; only 30% of activity attributed to the exemption; revised summary (Dec 2025, "Revised January 2026") attributes 8,505 construction and 1,641 operations jobs; construction fiscal ROI 4% — [DOAA Data Center Tax Exemption Summary (Revised)](https://www.audits2.ga.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Data-Center-Tax-Exemption-Summary-Revised.pdf)
- The original Dec 24, 2025 summary stated 28,350 construction jobs / $3.4 bn and 5,471 operations jobs / $823m; the correction reduced these to 8,505 / $1.0 bn and 1,641 / $247m; cost unchanged at $474m — [Augusta Press, 16 Jan 2026 (reporting DOAA correction)](https://theaugustapress.com/error-in-state-auditors-data-center-review-inflated-job-production-and-economic-value); earlier (2023) DOAA summary for comparison — [DOAA 2023 summary](https://www.audits2.ga.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Data-Center-Sales-Tax-Exemption-Summary.pdf)

**US / Texas — JETI (Ch. 403)**
- SAO Report 26-009 (Dec 11, 2025) audited selected JETI agreements; found Comptroller and two ISDs compliant (low risk), Governor's Office medium risk: should strengthen performance-bond verification and due-diligence reviews; only two executed agreements mentioned; no datacenter-specific figures — [Texas SAO 26-009](https://sao.texas.gov/SAOReports/ReportNumber?id=26-009)
- JETI application list (public) — [Comptroller JETI applications](https://comptroller.texas.gov/economy/development/prop-tax/jeti/applications.php) (not individually parsed)

**Brazil — Redata**
- Senate budget-adequacy note NT 33/2025 (23 Sept 2025): renúncia R$5.20 bn (2026), R$1.00 bn (2027), R$1.05 bn (2028) per Exposição de Motivos 00010/2025 (MDIC/MF/MME, 16 Sept 2025); PLOA 2026 table 11 p. 96; counterparts: ≥10% capacity to domestic market, 2% of acquisitions to R&D; no job figures — [Senado NT 33/2025](https://www12.senado.leg.br/orcamento/documentos/estudos/tipos-de-estudos/notas-tecnicas-de-adequacao-orcamentaria-e-financeira/mp-1318-2025-nota-tecnica-no-33-2025.pdf)
- MP 1.318/2025 lapsed; PL 278/2026 approved by Câmara 24 Feb 2026; Senate rapporteur parecer (Cid Gomes, 2026) repeats R$5.2/1.0/1.05 bn (attributed to Receita Federal), requires full demand from clean/renewable sources, 2% R&D with ≥40% to N/NE/CO, counterparts reduced 20% for N/NE/CO projects, introduces a water efficiency index without threshold; "no job figures" — [Parecer Cid Gomes (copy hosted by Poder360)](https://static.poder360.com.br/uploads/2026/09/Parecer-Cid-Gomes-Redata-Senado-1-se-2026.pdf); [Senado Sumário Executivo MP 1318](https://www12.senado.leg.br/publicacoes/estudos-legislativos/tipos-de-estudos/sumarios-de-proposicoes/mpv1318/@@download/file/Sumario_Executivo_MP1318.pdf)

**India — Uttar Pradesh Data Centre Policy 2026**
- G.O. 536/78-2-2026/E-105226 (9 July 2026): capital subsidy 7% of eligible fixed capital investment capped ₹20 Cr (₹2 Cr/yr), AI Compute booster +₹5 Cr for ≥350 FP64-grade units; park interest subsidy 60% for 7 years capped ₹50 Cr; land subsidy 50% (Bundelkhand/Purvanchal) / 25% elsewhere; 100% stamp duty exemption; 100% electricity duty exemption 10 years; 25-year wheeling-charge exemptions 50–100%; targets >₹2 lakh crore investment and 2 GW+; ≥50% renewable share target by 2030; no jobs target (applicants give projections) — [UP Data Centre Policy 2026 (Invest UP)](https://invest.up.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/policy_En.pdf)

**Malaysia — approvals and jobs**
- Digital Ministry written parliamentary reply (15 Oct 2026): 27 datacenter projects approved since the Feb 2025 Data Centre Task Force (16 Johor, 10 Selangor, 1 N. Sembilan), RM225.97 bn pipeline, 4,053 projected jobs incl. 3,523 high-skilled, 89.21% expected to go to Malaysians; ≥30% local content; MIDA monitors via six-monthly reports — [The Vibes, 15 Oct 2026 (reporting the written reply)](https://www.thevibes.com/articles/news/128358/twenty-seven-approved-data-centres-projected-to-create-4053-jobs-including-3523-skilled-roles)
- Minister Gobind Singh Deo, Dewan Rakyat 12 Nov 2024: RM141.72 bn digital investments approved Jan–Oct 2024; 41,078 jobs for the whole digital economy (not datacenters); 10 datacenters operating in Johor, 7 under development, 36 applications under study — [MIDA news reproducing Bernama](https://www.mida.gov.my/mida-news/rm141-7b-worth-of-digital-investments-approved-in-2024-41078-jobs-created-and-10-data-centres-operating-in-johor-says-gobind/)

### Inferences
- Georgia is the clearest documented case of a government body revising its own jobs attribution downward (by ~70%) while the cost figure stood.
- Across Brazil, UP and Malaysia, official subsidy instruments specify investment, R&D, local-content or renewable conditions but not enforceable job counts.

### Gaps
- EU state-aid decisions specific to datacenters were not found in this pass.
- Gulf sovereign funding figures: the WAM Stargate UAE article did not render; no official PIF/Humain capex figure retrieved.
- Texas Chapter 313 datacenter agreements not individually examined.

## Category 3 — Labor

### Takeaway
Jobs-per-site in official filings cluster around 50 FTE per 250,000 sq ft (JLARC), 170–260 FTE for a 96 MW UK campus (~2 FTE/MW), and ~150 jobs per approved Malaysian project. Documented labor conditions at the chain's other ends rest on court records (Kenya content moderators) and press reporting of an industrial accident (Morowali) for which official inspection findings were not retrievable.

### Cited Findings
- Virginia: typical 250,000 sq ft datacenter ≈50 full-time workers (half contractors), ≈1,500 peak construction; >8,000 operations jobs statewide FY23; avg pay ≈$100,000 — [JLARC Rpt 598, pp. 13–14](https://jlarc.virginia.gov/pdfs/reports/Rpt598.pdf)
- UK Abbots Langley (31 ha, 84,000 m², 96 MW IT): appellant's evidence 170–260 FTE (mid 210), wages £9–11m/yr, recorded by Inspector; officer report cited 201 FTE — [SoS decision 12 May 2025](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6821f977c66deec8488f7f42/Recovered_appeal_-_land_off_Bedmond_Road__Abbots_Langley.pdf); [Three Rivers officer report 23/1068/OUT](https://moderngov.threerivers.gov.uk/documents/s6537/231068OUT%20-%20Outline%20application%20Demolition%20and%20clearance%20of%20existing%20buildings%20and%20hardstandings%20.pdf)
- UK South Mimms DC01UK (85 acres ≈34.4 ha, up to 2m sq ft): 500 construction, 200 permanent, "10,900 indirect" claimed; 10% BNG; outline permission 23 Jan 2025 — [Hertsmere Borough Council news](https://www.hertsmere.gov.uk/news/data-centre-in-hertsmere-given-outline-permission-by-planning-committee)
- Malaysia: 4,053 jobs across 27 approved projects (≈150/project) — [see Category 2 source](https://www.thevibes.com/articles/news/128358/twenty-seven-approved-data-centres-projected-to-create-4053-jobs-including-3523-skilled-roles)
- Chile Alto Jahuel (DC Terranova, 92.7 MW substation, 16 ha property, 5.62 ha project area): sized for ~307 operating staff; SEA pertinencia (PERTI-2026-5962) found SEIA entry not required — [SEA Resolución, published 24 Jul 2026](https://firma.sea.gob.cl/publicaciones/2026/07/24/1784914495_42EC06A7-D626-4DCD-8456-E599CAEE7C3F)
- Kenya content moderators (Meta/Sama/Majorel): Court of Appeal, *Samasource EPZ Ltd v Meta Platforms Inc & 186 others* [2024] KECA 1152, 20 Sept 2024, allowed appeals and set aside the ELRC's 2 June 2023 interim orders (which had restrained redundancy, extended contracts, required medical/psychological care) because the trial judge "impermissibly and dangerously delved into contested issues of fact and the law"; merits remain for trial; jurisdiction over Meta not decided — [Kenya Law, [2024] KECA 1152](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/keca/2024/1152/eng@2024-09-20)
- Indonesia, ITSS furnace explosion at IMIP Morowali, 24 Dec 2023: IMIP spokesperson reported 18 dead (10 Indonesian, 8 Chinese) and 41 injured; police revised toll from 13 to 18 — [Jakarta Post, 27 Dec 2023](https://www.thejakartapost.com/id/business/2023/12/27/as-death-toll-rises-morowali-blast-brings-work-safety-squarely-into-focus); Tempo op-ed reports 21 dead and that Manpower Ministry investigators had difficulty accessing the park — [Tempo, Jan 2024](https://en.tempo.co/read/1819592/finding-blame-in-the-morowali-nickel-smelter-incident) (press, not official; older source)

### Inferences
- Official filings consistently state permanent jobs an order of magnitude below construction jobs and "indirect" claims.

### Gaps
- No ILO/Kemnaker official report on Morowali fatalities was retrieved; only press.
- Korean/Taiwanese labor-ministry occupational-disease statistics for fabs: search returned only press and NGO material; no official KCOMWEL dataset retrieved.
- No OECD/ILO document with current DRC artisanal figures retrieved (2019 OECD press coverage only).

## Category 4 — Land and biodiversity

### Takeaway
UK planning records give the most granular land/biodiversity data (hectares, Green Belt status, BNG % as a condition); Chile's SEA publishes site areas and protected-area distances; EU-level data exists for water (WUE) but not land.

### Cited Findings
- Abbots Langley, Herts (31 ha, Metropolitan Green Belt, active arable land): SoS allowed recovered appeal 12 May 2025; Parcel 1 found "grey belt" (NPPF para 155) so not inappropriate; Condition 11 requires ≥135% biodiversity net gain (Metric 4.0), largely delivered on Parcel 2 country park within the site; loss of agricultural land given limited weight; no heat-reuse condition — [SoS decision letter](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6821f977c66deec8488f7f42/Recovered_appeal_-_land_off_Bedmond_Road__Abbots_Langley.pdf); council leader said the Green Belt would get "mega warehouse-type buildings" and pledged to secure heat use — [Three Rivers DC, 13 May 2025](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/news/council-leader-responds-abbots-langley-data-centre)
- South Mimms DC01UK: 85 acres, 10% BNG, outline permission 23 Jan 2025 — [Hertsmere](https://www.hertsmere.gov.uk/news/data-centre-in-hertsmere-given-outline-permission-by-planning-committee)
- Virginia: ~7,200 acres statewide (~2,914 ha) for ~150 sites — [JLARC](https://jlarc.virginia.gov/pdfs/reports/Rpt598.pdf)
- Chile Alto Jahuel: 16 ha property, 5.62 ha project area, 3.21 ha built; nearest protected area (Parque de la Viña Santa Rita monument) ~1,200 m; nearest wetland HRU-13-43 ~1,476 m; 56 diesel generators 94.95 MW, ~990 t diesel (just under the 1,000 t SEIA threshold) — [SEA PERTI-2026-5962](https://firma.sea.gob.cl/publicaciones/2026/07/24/1784914495_42EC06A7-D626-4DCD-8456-E599CAEE7C3F)
- Chile Huechuraba AWS: 10.9 ha, 21,350 m² built — [Emol/Tribunal Ambiental](https://www.emol.com/noticias/Economia/2026/04/06/1196414/amazon-tribunal-ambiental.html)
- Zeewolde: 166 ha (80 ha state land) — [Eerste Kamer](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/nieuws/20220222/interpellatie_grootschalige)
- EU: first EED datacenter reporting (770 sites, ~36% of those in scope; six Member States reported none): EU average WUE 0.58 m³/MWh (range 0.07–1.28), PUE 1.36, 68 TWh in 2024 → 114 TWh (3.2% of EU demand) by 2030 — [COM(2026) 500 final, 21 Sept 2026](https://www.cdep.ro/afaceri_europene/CE/2026/COM_2026_500_EN_ACT_part1_v4.pdf)

### Inferences
- UK BNG conditions (135% at Abbots Langley) are enforceable planning conditions; no monitoring data yet exists because construction has not completed.

### Gaps
- No positive case with published biodiversity monitoring data (post-construction) was found in official sources.
- Global Forest Watch-documented forest conversion for a named datacenter was not found.

## Category 5 — Circularity

### Takeaway
Municipally owned heat networks (Stockholm Exergi 50% City-owned: 117 GWh recovered 2023; Tallaght/Heat Works 100% council-owned: ~6,000 MWh cumulative to June 2024) publish delivered-heat figures. EU-wide, only 67 of 770 reporting datacenters reuse any heat (~1.8%). Denmark now legally requires >1 MW datacenters to use surplus heat unless a cost-benefit analysis shows infeasibility. Microsoft's 90.9% hardware reuse/recycle figure is self-reported; the Deloitte limited-assurance review scope does not name it.

### Cited Findings
- Stockholm Exergi (owned 50% Stockholms stad, 50% Ankhiale): "Under 2023 stod värmeåtervinningen från Stockholm Data Parks och Öppen Fjärrvärme för 117 GWh" — [Stockholm Exergi Årsredovisning 2023](https://www.stockholmexergi.se/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Arsredovisning-2023_SV_240326_sidor.pdf)
- Tallaght District Heating (Heat Works, fully owned by South Dublin County Council; 100% of heat from Amazon datacenter): ~6,000 MWh generated to date and 1,098 tCO2 saved as of June 2024; ~1,500 tCO2/yr estimated; serves County Hall and TU Dublin Tallaght — [SEAI case study](https://www.seai.ie/case-studies/tallaght-district-heating); council reply 14 Apr 2025 confirms Heatworks as "Ireland's first publicly owned, not-for-profit energy company" — [South Dublin CC meeting reply](https://meetings.southdublin.ie/Home/ViewReply/85873)
- EU: only 67 datacenters reported any waste-heat reuse; ≈1.8% of heat reused (Commission cautions some values unrealistic); EU REF 0.86 — [COM(2026) 500](https://www.cdep.ro/afaceri_europene/CE/2026/COM_2026_500_EN_ACT_part1_v4.pdf)
- Denmark BEK nr. 1167 (2 Oct 2025, in force 11 Oct 2025): datacenters >1 MW must prepare a cost-benefit analysis (§3) and use surplus heat unless infeasible (§6); exemption procedure §5 — [Retsinformation BEK 1167/2025](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2025/1167/pdf)
- Microsoft: "90.9% reuse and recycling of cloud hardware servers and components" (FY24), goal 90% by 2025 — [2025 Environmental Sustainability Report](https://cdn-dynmedia-1.microsoft.com/is/content/microsoftcorp/microsoft/msc/documents/presentations/CSR/2025-Microsoft-Environmental-Sustainability-Report.pdf); method weight-based ((reuse kg + recycle kg × efficiency)/total kg), >3.2 million components reused FY24, no third-party verification mentioned — [Microsoft Circularity White Paper, Apr 2025](https://datacenters.microsoft.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/CircularityWhitePaperApril2025.pdf); Deloitte & Touche LLP limited-assurance review (AT-C 210), dated May 29, 2025, covers Section 1 of the Data Fact Sheet, which contains operational waste tonnages and packaging recyclability (94.8%) but no cloud-hardware reuse/recycle percentage — [2025 Environmental Data Fact Sheet](https://cdn-dynmedia-1.microsoft.com/is/content/microsoftcorp/microsoft/msc/documents/presentations/CSR/2025-Microsoft-Environmental-Data-Fact-Sheet-PDF.pdf); FY25 fact sheet (Deloitte review dated 9 July 2026) likewise contains no hardware reuse percentage; total waste 58,868 mt, diverted 36,149 mt — [2026 Environmental Data Fact Sheet](https://cdn-dynmedia-1.microsoft.com/is/content/microsoftcorp/microsoft/msc/documents/presentations/CSR/2026-Microsoft-Environmental-Data-Fact-Sheet-PDF.pdf)
