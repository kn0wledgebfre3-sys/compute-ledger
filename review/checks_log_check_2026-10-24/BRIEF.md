# Brief: is the corrections log honest? (24 October 2026)

An independent reader who did not build the log was asked to find anything in it that overstates, understates or misrepresents: the register's prose counts against the review files; whether every finding in every result file reached `findings.csv` with its verdict; 30 findings drawn at random (seed 7), each status verified against the current files; the random-sample paragraph recomputed and its classification checked against each check's brief; and the per-entry line for five entries.

Output: `check_result.csv` (file, location, what is wrong, evidence, suggested fix, severity). The `resolution` column was added when the findings were applied.
