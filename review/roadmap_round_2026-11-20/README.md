# Roadmap items, read twice (20 November 2026)

**What was read.** Three groups of items from the roadmap.
- **A. Morowali, Meta and Equinix** (from the check of the three views): the death tolls of the PT ITSS furnace explosion of 24 December 2023 in The Jakarta Post and on the Indonesian National Police's site; Meta's review reports from EY for 2024 (a dated copy) and for 2025, with the restatement of 2023 and 2024, and Meta's 2025 data index; Equinix's 2025 Sustainability Report.
- **B. Scotland, the register items still unread**: Chapelcross (the council's scoping opinion and the applicant's EIA Report), Wester Hermiston, Larbert (the council's minute of a pre-determination hearing and the applicant's planning statement), and five further proposals from council and applicant documents: Ravenscraig, DataVita DV4 at Chapelhall in the Lanarkshire AI Growth Zone, Spango Valley in Greenock, Southside near Duns and Aurelius at Newhouse. Several of these documents are copies hosted on a campaign group's website or on planning-data sites; the sites' notes say which.
- **C. Record-based counts of datacentres** in EPA's Facility Registry Service and the Environment Agency's public register.

**How.** For A and B, a first reader found the documents and took the items (`BRIEF_A_first.md`, `BRIEF_B_first.md`, `readers/A_first_reading.csv`, `readers/B_first_reading.csv`). A second reader was given each item's document and question, not the first reader's value (`BRIEF_A_second.md`, `BRIEF_B_second.md`, `readers/*_second_reading_tasks.csv`), and read them independently (`readers/*_second_reading_result.csv`). The two readings were compared item by item (`check_result.csv`). For C there was one reading (`BRIEF_C_first.md`, `readers/C_first_reading.csv`): no count could be obtained, so nothing entered and there was nothing to read twice.

**Result.** 111 items agree and are used; 32 agree but are not used (the reason is given for each); 7 differ and are not used; 16 could not be opened by a reader; 99 were read once only and are not used: reprints and press accounts of counts read in the police's or the park's own documents, chart labels, company documents not cited for these figures, and the attempts at the counts in C.

**What entered the ledger.**
- **Morowali.** The 18 deaths the ledger described as the industrial park's initial count is the police's count of 26 December 2023: The Jakarta Post writes that 'The police also revised the death toll up from 13 to 18', and the police's own article of that day says the toll 'has risen to 18 this afternoon. It was 13 yesterday'. The park's first count, in its statement of 24 December, was 13 (eight Indonesian and five Chinese workers) with 38 injured. Added as records: the police's counts of 25 December (13), 26 December (18) and 2 January 2024 (20, with 12 Indonesian and eight foreign workers, 29 severely injured and 11 with minor injuries), beside the 21 of 4 January already held. Added as claims: the park's counts of 24 December and of 26 December. Four figure ids changed (`data/climate_trace_seed/measurement_id_aliases.csv`), and the newspaper's document page moved to its English address (`sites/views/document_id_aliases.csv`).
- **Meta.** The 2024 assured figures now link to the dated copy of EY's review for 2024 on Meta's site, because the address first cited serves the review for 2025 (six ids changed). EY's two reviews for 2025 (30 October 2026, limited assurance) are added as records, with the restated Scope 1 and market-based Scope 2 for 2023 and 2024; the earlier figures stay, each with its document, and their notes give the restated values. The 2025 data index's figures for 2024 carbon credits and renewable matching are added as claims, which restores two same-year pairs on the records-beside-claims page.
- **Equinix.** Four 2025 figures from its Sustainability Report, as claims: the report says the figures are assured but names no assurer or level, and the assurance statement and the 2025 Data Summary could not be opened.
- **Scotland.** Larbert's applicant (Apatura DC Project 5 Ltd) and position (from the applicant's grid reference); Chapelcross's site area from the council's scoping opinion and its standby generators from the applicant's EIA Report; five new sites with their applicants' figures (claims) and, for Ravenscraig, the council's screening opinion (a record). Spango Valley and Southside are placed from the grid references in their applicants' documents; Ravenscraig is not placed, because its applicant's request prints two different eastings for the same parcel.

**Not entered, and why.** Westerhill, Bishopbriggs: the second reader could not open its case page. The SPICe article does not name Wester Hermiston, so it is not cited for that decision. Ravenscraig's floor area is described in two ways in the same document. DataVita's '120MVA total load' is in the site's notes, not the figures table: the table's figures in MVA are connection, contract and approval capacities, and none of its capacity measures is a site's planned total load in MVA. The counts in C are on the roadmap with what was learned about each register.
