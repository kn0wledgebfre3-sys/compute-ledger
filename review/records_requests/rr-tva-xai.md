## Subject

FOIA request: Power Availability memoranda (Feb. 11 and Aug. 20, 2026), CTC Property LLC term sheet and agreements, system impact studies

## Body

To the TVA FOIA Officer:

Under the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. § 552, I request electronic copies of:

1. The "Confidential Memorandum dated January 26, 2026," filed as Exhibit 02/11/26E to the Board's February 11, 2026 Power Availability resolution.
2. The memorandum dated July 22, 2026, filed as Exhibit 08/20/2026P to the Board's August 20, 2026 Power Availability resolution.
3. The term sheet and the demand-response agreement with CTC Property LLC (xAI) referred to in the October 7, 2024 memorandum supporting the Board's November 7, 2024 resolution on a greater-than-100 MW firm power arrangement with CTC Property LLC.
4. TVA's system impact studies for the first and second 150 MW requests at the former Electrolux site, 3231 Paul R. Lowry Rd, Memphis.

Please release all reasonably segregable portions. For anything withheld, please identify the record or passage and the exemption claimed. I note that TVA released the 150 MW figure in the November 2024 Board materials.

For fees, I am in the "all other requesters" category; please tell me before fees exceed $25. If the request is large, I am glad to narrow it; items 1 and 2 are the priority.
