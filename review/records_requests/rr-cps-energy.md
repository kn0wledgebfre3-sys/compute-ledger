## Subject

Public Information Act request: large-load queue, contracted large loads and data-center sales (March 4, 2026 Board update)

## Body

To CPS Energy Open Records:

Under the Texas Public Information Act, Gov't Code ch. 552, I request electronic copies of:

1. The large-load queue as of January 12, 2026 underlying the March 4, 2026 Board informational update "Managing Large Load Growth Responsibly" (59 projects, 26,146 MW requested), showing each applicant and its requested MW.
2. The list of "Contracted" large-load projects (335 MW as of 01/12/2026), with customer name, site address and contracted MW.
3. The Large Load Interconnection Study and End-User Interconnection Study reports for any Microsoft Corporation or CyrusOne site.
4. Annual kWh and peak billing kW for 2023, 2024 and 2025 for accounts on the Large Power or Extra Large Power rate at Microsoft's Westover Hills and Texas Research Park sites.
5. Annual kWh sales to large-load or data-center customers for 2023 to 2025, in aggregate, as used for the statement that 9% of commercial sales come from large load.

If any part is withheld, please identify it and the exemption claimed, and release the rest, for example the queue with MW even if names are withheld. If the request is large, I am glad to narrow it; items 1 and 2 come first.
