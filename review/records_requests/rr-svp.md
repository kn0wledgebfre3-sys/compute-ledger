## Subject

Public Records Act request: Silicon Valley Power data-center load and sales, and large-load requests

## Body

To the City Clerk, City of Santa Clara:

Under the California Public Records Act, Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq., I request electronic copies of the following Silicon Valley Power records:

1. The FY2024 and FY2025 summary of sales by rate schedule (kWh and peak kW), including data-center and large-industrial schedules, as used for the "60% of power use and sales from data centers" slide in "AI Data Center Power Supply and Impacts" (NCPA Annual Conference, September 25, 2025).
2. The values by year behind the "Actual Demand vs. Agreement Demand (in MW)" chart in the same presentation.
3. The "Under Contract / In Negotiations / In Pipeline (MWh)" load-growth table in the same presentation.
4. SVP's list of its ten largest customers by annual kWh for FY2024 and FY2025.
5. The list of pending requests for new or increased data-center service (site, requested MW, date of request) behind the presentation's reference to a "request to add 500+ MW load."

If any part is withheld, please identify it and the exemption claimed, and release the rest, for example the figures without customer names. If the request is large, I am glad to narrow it.

Thank you,
