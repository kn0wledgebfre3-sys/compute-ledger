# rr-oppd-nppd-les: OPPD, NPPD and Lincoln Electric System, large-customer and data-center records

Three separate filings, one per utility. Each has its own subject and body below.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Statute | Nebraska Public Records Statutes, Neb. Rev. Stat. §§ 84-712 to 84-712.09 |
| Deadline | § 84-712(4): as soon as practicable and without delay, and not more than 4 business days after receipt: provide the records, deny in writing, or explain the delay in writing with the earliest practicable date. OPPD's page states the same 4 business days. |
| Requester restriction | None in the statute ("all citizens of this state and all other persons interested"). But OPPD gives the first 8 hours of staff time free only to Nebraska residents and news media, so a Nebraska-resident filer pays less. |
| Expected exemptions (notes) | § 84-712.05(3) (trade secrets / proprietary commercial information). |

## A. OPPD

### Subject

Public records request: large-customer kWh, data-center service requests and electric service agreements

### Body

To OPPD Legal Operations:

Under Neb. Rev. Stat. §§ 84-712 to 84-712.09, I request electronic copies of:

1. OPPD's annual "Large Customer" or "Top 10 customers" kWh report for 2023, 2024 and 2025.
2. The list of service requests of 1 MW or more received since January 1, 2024, including the 19 written requests from data centers reported in November 2024, with requested MW and date.
3. The Board of Directors "All Committees" or "Energy Production & Delivery" committee presentations from 2024 to date whose load-forecast slides attribute growth to data centers, with their MW figures.
4. The electric service agreements with Meta Platforms (Papillion) and Google LLC (Papillion and Omaha) showing contract demand.

If any part is withheld, please identify it and the exemption claimed, and release the rest. If the request is large, I am glad to narrow it.

Thank you,
[Name], [email]
