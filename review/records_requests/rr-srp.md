## Subject

Public records request: data-center load, E-67 accounts and large-load requests

## Body

To the Salt River Project Agricultural Improvement and Power District, Public Records:

Under Arizona's public records law, A.R.S. § 39-121 et seq., I request electronic copies of:

1. The 2025 summer system peak (MW) and the data-center load at that hour that underlie the statement on SRP's "Data Centers and SRP" page that data centers "contributed 5.1% to SRP's summer 2025 peak demand record."
2. The 2025 price-process filings and Board or Council presentations that show kWh and billing demand for the Large General Service / E-67 class.
3. The list of E-67 accounts of 20 MW or more, with their forecast load.
4. The electric service agreements with Meta Platforms, Google LLC and Apple Inc. for their Mesa sites, showing contract capacity.
5. The current list of pending large-load service requests (site, requested MW, requested in-service date).

This request is not for a commercial purpose.

If any part is withheld, please identify it and the reason claimed, and release the rest, for example the figures without customer names. If the request is large, I am glad to narrow it.

Thank you,
[Name], [email]
