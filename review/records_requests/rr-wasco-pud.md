## Subject

Public records request: Google LLC load data and electric service agreements, The Dalles

## Body

To the Public Records Custodian, Northern Wasco County PUD:

Under the Oregon Public Records Law, ORS 192.311 to 192.478, I request electronic copies of:

1. Annual kWh and peak kW for 2015 through 2025 for the accounts of Google LLC and Design LLC at The Dalles.
2. The electric service agreements with Google LLC or Design LLC, including the contract demand.
3. The PUD's records for the Google campus under BPA's New Large Single Load (NLSL) rules, including any NLSL determinations and related correspondence with BPA.
4. The "major customer" concentration note in the PUD's audited financial statements for the three most recent fiscal years.

If any part is withheld, please identify it and the exemption claimed, and release the rest. If fees will exceed $25, please send an estimate first. If the request is large, I am glad to narrow it; items 1 and 2 are the priority.

Thank you,
[Name], [email]
