## Subject

Public Information Act request: virtual currency mining facility registrations under 16 TAC § 25.114

## Body

To the Public Utility Commission of Texas:

Under the Texas Public Information Act, Gov't Code ch. 552, I request electronic copies of records of the virtual currency mining facilities registered with the Commission under PURA § 39.360 and 16 TAC § 25.114 (Project No. 56962), from the rule's adoption in 2024 to the date of your search:

1. A list or export of the registrations showing, for each facility, the registrant, county, registered load (MW) and registration date.
2. If the Commission withholds facility-level detail, any existing record showing the number of registered facilities and their total registered load (MW), such as a summary, report or tally from the registration tool, by county or year if available.

If any part is withheld, please identify it and the exemption claimed, and release the rest; at a minimum, I ask for the number of registered facilities and their total MW. If the request is large, I am glad to narrow it.

Thank you,
[Name], [email]
