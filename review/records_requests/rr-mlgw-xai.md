## Subject

Public records request: CTC Property LLC (xAI) service agreement, system impact studies and billing data, 3231 Paul R. Lowry Rd

## Body

To the MLGW Public Records Coordinator:

[Filer: this must be filed by a Tennessee citizen, who states that here and shows Tennessee ID as MLGW requires.] Under the Tennessee Public Records Act, Tenn. Code Ann. § 10-7-503, I request electronic copies of:

1. The executed Electric Service Agreement or power contract between MLGW and CTC Property LLC for 3231 Paul R. Lowry Rd, Memphis, including the contract demand (kW) and any curtailment or demand-response exhibit.
2. The MLGW and TVA system impact studies for the first and second 150 MW service requests at Paul R. Lowry Rd, referred to in MLGW's "xAI Update" of May 5, 2025.
3. Monthly billing demand (kW) and energy (kWh) for each CTC Property LLC account from June 2024 to the date of your search.
4. The "finalized request for grid power" for the Tulane Rd site and any system impact study for it.
5. The work order or cost estimate for the Electrolux substation upgrade shown as $760,000 in the July 9, 2024 presentation "Utility Support to xAI" to the City Council MLGW Committee.

If any part is withheld, please identify it and the exemption claimed, and release the rest. If the request is large, I am glad to narrow it; items 1 and 3 are the priority. I will provide proof of Tennessee citizenship as your policy requires.

Thank you,
[Name], [email]
