## Subject

Public records request (RCW 42.56): retail load by rate schedule, data-center loads, large-load queue and Microsoft agreement

## Body

To the Public Records Officer:

Under the Public Records Act, RCW 42.56, I request electronic copies of:

1. Retail load by rate schedule for 2024 and 2025 (aMW, MWh and peak kW), including Rate Schedules 7, 14 and 17.
2. Monthly load (kWh and peak kW) for 2024 and 2025, by customer, for each of the eight data-center operators referred to in "Grant PUD & Data Centers: FAQs" (August 28, 2026).
3. The large-load queue (applicant, location, requested MW) as of the latest date available, as summarized in the August 27, 2024 commission recap (2,897 MW requested by 75 applicants).
4. The power sales or electric service agreements with Microsoft Corporation for Quincy, showing contract demand.
5. The per-customer growth allocations ("megawatts they have left to grow") set by the commission's March 25, 2025 action.

If any part is withheld, please identify it and the exemption claimed, and release the rest. If the request is large, I am glad to narrow it or receive it in installments; items 1 and 3 come first.

Thank you,
[Name], [email]
