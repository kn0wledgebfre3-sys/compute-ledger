# Your part (C): national energy, water and public money

1. **Climate Change Agreements**: the datacentre sector has its own Climate Change Agreement. Find the official results (the Environment Agency's or the Department for Energy Security and Net Zero's reports on CCA target periods, and the sector's published commitment document): the sector's energy use, its improvement target and whether it was met, the number of facilities, any PUE-based figure. techUK administers the sector; its own publications are the industry's statements (claims), the agency's and department's are records.
2. **National demand estimates**: NESO's Future Energy Scenarios (2024, 2025) and any DESNZ or Ofgem document that states datacentre electricity demand now and projected (say which scenario and year). These are estimates by the system operator: record them as such.
3. **Water**: statutory water resources management plans (2024) of Thames Water, Affinity Water, Anglian Water and others, or Environment Agency documents, that state water demand from datacentres. (Affinity Water's evidence to Parliament is already held.)
4. **Public money and government designations**: AI Growth Zones (Culham, the North East, Wales, and others announced), the government's compute programme, and datacentres' designation as Critical National Infrastructure (September 2024). Government announcements of investment and jobs are claims; record them as claims, and look for any record behind them (a National Audit Office report, a Public Accounts Committee or select committee report, a published business case or grant agreement, a planning decision). Also any National Audit Office or committee report on datacentres or national compute (for example the AI Research Resource or Isambard-AI funding).

Write `result_C.csv` in this folder. Pace yourself: about 55 web searches in all.
