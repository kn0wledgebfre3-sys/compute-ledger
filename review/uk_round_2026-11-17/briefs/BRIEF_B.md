# Your part (B): planning decisions on large datacenters

Decisions from 2023 to November 2026 on large datacenter applications in the UK: local planning authority committee reports and decision notices, the Planning Inspectorate's appeal decisions and the Secretary of State's decision letters (recovered appeals and call-ins), and Scottish and Welsh equivalents. Look in particular for applications in Buckinghamshire (Iver), Slough, Hillingdon, Hertfordshire (other than South Mimms and Abbots Langley), Essex (Harlow), Northumberland (Cambois, Blyth), Teesside, Oxfordshire (Culham), Lincolnshire, and Wales (Newport), and any others of similar size.

For each, record what the documents state: applicant, site, decision and date, floor area, site area, IT or electrical capacity in MW, grid connection, number of backup generators, water supply or cooling, jobs during operation and construction (and who claims them: an applicant's figure quoted in a committee report is the applicant's claim, an inspector's finding is a record), Green Belt or grey belt status, and any figure for biodiversity net gain. Say whether a figure is the applicant's or the authority's.

Write `result_B.csv` in this folder. Pace yourself: about 55 web searches in all.
