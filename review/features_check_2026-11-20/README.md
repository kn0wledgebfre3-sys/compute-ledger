# Totals, the map, what changed, link previews and correction links: checked twice by two checkers (20 November 2026)

**What was checked.** The parts added on 20 November 2026: `totals.html` (totals that can be added), the richer `map.html` and `data/sites.json`, `changes.html` and `feed.xml`, the link previews and canonical addresses on every page, the correction and record links in every footer, the per-figure "Cite or report" links, and the link that loads the main tables into the reader's browser to query with SQL.

**How.** Two checkers who did not build the pages worked side by side, and each checked twice: once on the first build, and again after the fixes, the second time also on the data changed in the same round (`review/roadmap_round_2026-11-20/`).
- **Accuracy.** Recomputed every total from the ledger's tables under the page's own rules, with every figure added, left out (and why), of another definition and of another period; the map's per-topic counts, status classes and organisations' sites against the tables and the organisations' pages; the changes page and feed against the changelog; the link previews and footer links on pages of every kind; and the new figures against both readings. Files: `checkers/accuracy_first.csv`, `checkers/accuracy_second.csv`.
- **Browser.** Opened the pages at desktop and phone widths, light and dark, and tested every filter, the address kept in each, the map's popups, keyboard use and focus, the totals' lists, the forms the footer links fill, forwarding pages and the share picture. Files: `checkers/browser_first.csv`, `checkers/browser_second.csv` (screenshots were not kept).

**Result.** 83 problems over the two checks (4 errors, 8 inaccuracies, 27 wording, 7 layout, 7 behaviour, 30 polish); `check_result.csv` lists each with how it was resolved. Among them: the totals added figures their filings call estimated, flagged as part-year or of an unconfirmed year, and one partly estimated water figure (all now left out, with the reason); the map counted sites with only computed figures as having records (the legend now says so); the front page and the brief had no footer links; a document page's address changed with nothing at the old one (it now forwards). Three are on the roadmap: placeholder site names in the sites' own records, a New York City dataset cited at addresses that differ only in capital letters (five document pages for one source), and an undated address for Meta's 2025 reviews.
