# Singapore

Sites and figures from Singapore's government records: IMDA's and EDB's datacentre calls for application and their awards, the Green Data Centre Roadmap, parliamentary replies, and JTC's Jurong Island park. The allocation calls name the companies awarded capacity, not their facilities, so most sites have no location. What was read and what is missing is in `data/climate_trace_seed/SINGAPORE.md`.
