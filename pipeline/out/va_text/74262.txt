1. Emission Controls (SCR) – Nitrogen oxide (NO ) emissions from the non-emergency
X
engine gen-sets (Ref. Nos. BPEG1 through BPEG22) shall be controlled by closed loop
Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR). Each SCR system shall be equipped with a
temperature probe to continuously monitor and record the catalyst bed exhaust temperature
while the engine gen-set is operational. Engine exhaust gas shall be treated with urea,
except for periods of start-up, shutdown, or malfunction, when the catalyst bed exhaust
temperature of 575°F is achieved.
The permittee shall operate the engine gen-set and SCR such that the catalyst bed exhaust
temperature does not exceed 1,022°F. The SCR shall be provided with adequate access for
inspection and shall be in operation when the engine gen-sets are operating as stated above.
