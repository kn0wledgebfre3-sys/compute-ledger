emergency diesel engine gen-set (Ref. Nos. 1 through 75) and control device according to

4. Operating Limitations (Ozone Season) – No emergency diesel engine gen-set (Ref. Nos.

permittee may petition the Regional Air Compliance Manager of the DEQ’s NRO for
exceptions to this requirement, with approvals made on a case-by-case basis.

operations to posture the system in a more reliable state; or
