PROCESS REQUIREMENTS
1. Emission Controls (SCR) – Nitrogen oxide (NO ) emissions from the emergency diesel
X
engine gen-sets (Ref. Nos. 1 through 57) shall be controlled by closed loop Selective
Catalytic Reduction (SCR). Each SCR system shall be equipped with a temperature probe
to continuously monitor the catalyst bed exhaust temperature while the engine gen-set is
operational. Engine exhaust gas shall be treated with urea, except for periods of start-up,
shutdown, or malfunction, when:
a. The catalyst bed exhaust temperature of 540°F is achieved and
b. The engine gen-set is operating at or above ten percent (10%) generator load or 300
ekW (electrical kilowatt) output from the generator.
The permittee shall operate the engine gen-set and SCR such that the catalyst bed exhaust
temperature does not exceed 977°F. The SCR shall be provided with adequate access for
inspection and shall be in operation when the engine gen-sets are operating as stated above.
(9VAC5-80-1180 and 9VAC5-50-260) [1/20/2026]
