volatile organic compound emissions and nitrogen oxide emissions shall be controlled by the
use of good operating practices and performing appropriate maintenance in accordance with
the manufacturer recommendations. In addition, the permittee may only change those
settings that are permitted by the manufacturer which donot increase air emissions.
