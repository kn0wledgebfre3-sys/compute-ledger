The staff of the Virginia Department of Environmental Quality (DEQ) has reviewed the permit
application dated September 27, 2000 regarding the installation and operation of two Caterpillar
model 3508B diesel-fired generators, each rated at 1000 kilowatts (kW). The emergency
generators are located at the Exodus Communications, Incorporated facility (DC1) in Herndon,
Fairfax County, Virginia.
This permit for installation and operation of the proposed emergency generators is approved based
upon the information submitted. The generators are to be used only for providing power at the
location during interruption of service from the normal power supplier, periodic maintenance
testing, and operational training. Each generator shall operate not more than 300 hours per year.
Operational records shall be maintained on site which demonstrate that this hours-of-operation
limit continues to be observed. (Section 9 VAC 5-170-160 of the Regulations for the Control and
Abatement of Air Pollution). Visible emissions shall not exceed 20% opacity, except during one
six-minute period in any one hour in which visible emissions shall not exceed 30% opacity.
(Section 9 VAC 5-50-80 of the Regulations for the Control and Abatement of Air Pollution). Failure
to comply with these terms may result in a Notice of Violation and civil penalty.
This approval should not be construed to mean your entire operation is automatically in
compliance with all aspects of the Regulations. Regional personnel will be constantly evaluating all
sources for compliance with the Regulations.
In the event of any change in control or ownership of the permitted source, the permittee
shall notify the succeeding owner of the existence of this permit by letter and send a copy of that
letter to the Director, Northern Virginia Regional Office. (Section 9 VAC 5-170-160 of the
Regulations for the Control and Abatement of Air Pollution.
