INTRODUCTION
This permit approval is based on the permit application dated April 17, 2007, and
additional information dated June 7, 2007 and September 28, 2007. Any changes in the
permit application specifications or any existing facilities which alter the impact of the
facility on air quality may require a permit. Failure to obtain such a permit prior to
construction may result in enforcement action.
Words or terms used in this permit shall have meanings as provided in 9 VAC 5-80-
1110 (definitions) and 9 VAC 5-10-20 ofthe State Air Pollution Control Board's (Board)
Regulations for the Control and Abatement of Air Pollution (Regulations). The
regulatory reference or authority for each condition is listed in parentheses () after each
condition.

emissions data will necessitate a prompt response by the permittee to requests by the
Department of Environmental Quality (DEQ) or the Board for information to include, as
appropriate: process and production data; changes in control equipment; and operating
schedules. Such requests for information from the DEQ will either be in writing or by
personal contact.

applicable provisions ofthe Freedom of Information Act, §§ 2.2-3700 through 2.2-3714
